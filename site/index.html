The Collection

XOVRATH is an experimental collection of 2,222 ERC-721 artifacts on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet. Each one is a flat, two-dimensional abstract form — a fragment of something that was never a thing. Six classifications sort the unknown: VOID, DISTORTION, ORIGIN, ANOMALY, FRACTURE and UNKNOWN.

Minting is agent-only. There is no public sale page with a button. You talk to your agent, your agent talks to the chain, and the form finds its owner. How minting works →
