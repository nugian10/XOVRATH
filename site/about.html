About — Philosophy & Direction

Unknown Forms.
Undefined Existence.

CONTRACT NOT DEPLOYED YET — MINTING OPENS AFTER DEPLOY

XOVRATH is not a picture of something. It is a record of forms that were never things — shapes caught between being and not being, rendered flat, in two dimensions, because a third would have made them too certain.

Each of the 2,222 artifacts is generated from the same unknown source and sorted into one of six classifications. The classification does not explain the form. It only records how the form refused to be explained.

The work is deliberately restrained: black or bone-white ground, charcoal and silver masses, thin lines, a single circle or shard of violet where the form breaks its own rules. Grain over everything, like dust on a lens that was never cleaned.

The Six Classifications

— VOID

Forms defined by what is missing. The absence is the shape.

— DISTORTION

Forms that bend the space around them. Straight lines give up.

— ORIGIN

Forms that look like the beginning of something. Of what, unknown.

— ANOMALY

Forms that should not be in the set, and know it.

— FRACTURE

Forms broken along lines that were never drawn.

— UNKNOWN

Forms that fit no classification. The classification for the rest.
